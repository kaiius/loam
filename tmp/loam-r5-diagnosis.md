## TOP-5 TERRIBLE LIST

### 1. The main thread does up to ~120ms of synchronous sim per frame — that IS the jank
**What:** `frame()` in `page-template.html` runs up to 12 `doTick()` calls back-to-back inside a single `requestAnimationFrame` callback, then renders. I measured one tick at **~9.9ms in Node with only 34 creatures alive** (seed 7, steady state, 120-tick warmup). 12 ticks × 9.9ms ≈ **120ms of uninterrupted JS — seven dropped frames on a 60Hz display.** The 48-tick terrain repaint (~78ms+ by the code's own comment, measured at dpr1 — worse at phone dpr2) lands inside the same callback. Every pointer/key event queues behind these long tasks.
**Where:** `~/workspace/loam-artifact/page-template.html` — `frame()` L675–693 (`while (tickAcc >= 1 && n < 12)`), `doTick()` L655–673; `~/workspace/canopy-v020/src/material/render.js` — terrain cache repaint L1012–1060, `paintTerrain()` L2948.
**Why it's terrible (player terms):** this is "stuttering movement, laggy input, page stutter" with a name. Any hitch (a repaint, a GC pause, a tab-switch) banks `tickAcc`, and the next frame pays it back as a burst of up to 12 ticks — the hitch *grows*. At ⏩ (48 t/s) the sim alone needs ~8ms of every 16.6ms frame budget before a single pixel is drawn, so fast mode degrades into hitch-and-shed: the `n == 12` cap silently *drops* sim time, meaning ⏩ doesn't even run the world faster, it runs it in lurches. The "shed overload, never spiral" comment frames this as a design choice; from the player's chair it's the game freezing.
**A fix would need to touch:** tick budgeting in `frame()` (fewer ticks per frame, time-sliced ticks, or a worker for the sim); the repaint cost (smaller paint region, cheaper lightmap bake, dpr-aware paint sizing); and getting input handling off the same synchronous path as the sim burst.

### 2. Every creature is a dark blob at the size the page actually shows them
**What:** The page shows `VIEW_H = 560` world-px vertically (`page-template.html` L368). I measured actual creature heights: tanglekin 46, beetle 29, grub 41, jungle-cat 51 world-px — **5–9% of screen height**. The species art in `fauna.js` is genuinely differentiated (I read all six body plans: quadruped params, bird wings with primary notches, beetle elytra with center split, flutter bowtie wings, grub peristalsis), but every distinguishing feature is 1–3 screen px at game zoom — invisible. Worse, every species shares the same near-black dorsal palette (`palette()` in `fauna.js` L~40: top L=18, mid L=30) drawn on dark soil, so there is no luminance separation: dark smudges on a dark ground. My phone/desktop stills confirm it — at game zoom the roster is small dark pills; the art only becomes readable at 3–4× zoom (see `r5-closeup.png`). And the UI never tells you what you're looking at: the inspector header hardcodes `🐒 creature #${c.id}`, census rows hardcode `🐒 #${c.id}` — the word "species" appears nowhere in the interface.
**Where:** `~/workspace/loam-artifact/page-template.html` — `VIEW_H` L368, `renderCreaturePanel()` L473–512, `renderCensusPanel()` L544–562; `~/workspace/canopy-v020/src/material/fauna.js` — `drawSpeciesBody()` L594, `palette()` L~40.
**Why it's terrible (player terms):** Joshua said "all creatures look like the same ugly blobs" — he's right, and it's not because the art is one blob, it's because the *presentation* guarantees blobs: too small to read, too dark to separate from the ground, and never named. Eleven species shipped; the player experiences one.
**A fix would need to touch:** creature read at game zoom (larger on-screen presence and/or a closer default zoom, stronger silhouette contrast and backlight/rim separation from the ground rather than multiply-grading them *into* the darkness), and surfacing the species name in the inspector, census, and status line.

### 3. The terrain cache shows you the WRONG world after 🌱, and hitches rhythmically forever
**What:** Two defects in one cache. (a) `newWorld()` never clears `terrainCache`, and the repaint condition is purely geometric (`inside`) plus a tick delta — `cacheKey()` includes the seed but is **computed and never compared** (dead code, `render.js` L184–190). Re-grow the same seed and the camera lands in the same place, `inside` is true, and `mw.tick` was reset to 600 — so the *previous world's* paint keeps blitting until the tick counter climbs 48 past the old paint tick (if the old world painted at tick 650, that's ~98 ticks ≈ 8s at 12 t/s of looking at a world that no longer exists). (b) Even when working as designed, the 48-tick cadence forces a full multi-canvas repaint — sky canvas + world canvas + lightmap bake + tree canvas + composite + vignette, roughly 3 megapixels at dpr1 desktop and ~12MP at dpr2 phone — **every 4 seconds at 12 t/s and every 1 second at 48 t/s**. A rhythmic full-scene hitch is architected in.
**Where:** `~/workspace/canopy-v020/src/material/render.js` — cache check L1012–1060, `CACHE_TICKS` L179, `cacheKey()` L184–190, `paintTerrain()` L2948; `~/workspace/loam-artifact/page-template.html` — `newWorld()` L315–352 (no cache invalidation).
**Why it's terrible (player terms):** pressing "grow a new world" and seeing the old world for eight seconds reads as broken, full stop. And the repaint cadence means the page can never feel smooth — there is a scheduled stutter every few seconds by construction, worst exactly when the player asked for fast mode.
**A fix would need to touch:** cache invalidation on world (re)birth (clear the cache or actually compare the key); decoupling repaint cadence from the tick counter; making repaints cheaper (paint at display size, incremental updates, or dpr-capped paint canvases).

### 4. Camera controls are dead while paused, and touch has no pinch zoom
**What:** `bZoomIn`, `bZoomOut`, `bHome` (L769–777) mutate camera state but never call `render()` — and `frame()` only renders while `state.playing`. So **while paused, zoom, home, and drag-pan visibly do nothing**; the state changes apply silently whenever you next step or play, which feels like the buttons are broken. Zoom exists only as buttons and mouse-wheel — there is no pinch gesture, and the viewport meta sets `user-scalable=no`, so on the phone (the device Joshua actually uses) pinch does literally nothing. Related: the 🔬 Lab view always instruments the *founder* (`render()` L620–630 passes `creatureOpt(founder)`), ignoring whatever creature the player just clicked; and the ⋯ overflow menu doesn't close when you tap an item inside it.
**Where:** `~/workspace/loam-artifact/page-template.html` — L769–786 (camera cluster + overflow menu), `frame()` L675–693, `render()` L588–652, pointer handlers L800–870.
**Why it's terrible (player terms):** "things don't work as they should." Pause the game to look around — the most natural thing in a living-world toy — and every camera control is inert. On the phone, the primary zoom gesture the platform teaches doesn't exist. The Lab button promises instruments on *this* creature and shows you a different one.
**A fix would need to touch:** render-on-camera-change independent of play state (or a cheap camera-only render path); a two-pointer pinch handler; Lab view bound to the current selection; auto-closing the overflow menu on item tap.

### 5. The inspector and census rebuild their entire DOM on every tick
**What:** `refreshPanel(false)` runs inside `render()` every frame; whenever `mw.tick` changed it re-sets `panel.innerHTML` wholesale and rebinds every button handler — **12 full rebuilds/second at normal speed, 48/second at ⏩**, for as long as any panel is open. The census re-renders every row per tick. This is constant DOM parse + layout + handler churn stacked on top of the sim and canvas work, exactly when the page is busiest.
**Where:** `~/workspace/loam-artifact/page-template.html` — `refreshPanel()` L565–586, `renderCreaturePanel()` L473, `renderCensusPanel()` L544, `renderCellPanel()` L514, `renderPlantPanel()` L531.
**Why it's terrible (player terms):** opening the census — the thing you do when the world is most alive — makes the jank worse. It's a self-inflicted wound: nothing in that panel needs 48Hz updates; drives "moving as the chemistry ticks" don't need to be re-parsed from an HTML string 48 times a second.
**A fix would need to touch:** panel update cadence (cap at 2–4Hz, or update only changed values via `textContent` instead of `innerHTML`), and re-rendering the census list only when the roster actually changes.

## Anything else worth noting (not top-5)

- **The founder's species is `undefined`.** `spawnSpecies()` sets `c.species`, but `addFounder()` (`src/material/index.js` L114) never does — the founder renders as a tanglekin only by accident of the dispatch fallback (`oc2.drawing ? drawGrownBody : drawCreature`, `render.js` L1082–1085). Anything that keys off species (labels, census filters, future UI) will mis-handle the player's main creature.
- **The bear never spawns.** `LOAM_ROSTER` and `LOAM_CAPS` include `bear`, but `seedEcology()` (`src/material/species.js` L69–114) has no `put('bear', …)` line — its art path (`QUAD.bear`) is dead code in practice.
- **Speeds 3/6/24 are unreachable on the phone** — the `#bSpeed` cycler is `display:none` ≤640px, so mobile gets only 12 and 48. Minor, but the desktop UI advertises five speeds.
- **Zoom persists across new worlds** (`newWorld()` doesn't reset `state.zoom`) — growing a new world at 4× zoom drops you into a keyhole view of a world you haven't seen.
- **Wheel-zoom has dead code and does layout per tick:** `const scale = …` is computed and never used, and every wheel event calls `getBoundingClientRect()` (forced synchronous layout) plus mutates camera state through `screenToWorld → viewRect`.
- **Double-clicking empty ground both opens the cell panel and triggers ⌂ home** — the two clicks select first, then `dblclick` recenters. The panel and the camera disagree about what you meant.
- **The Lab button's label always reads "Lab"** even in inspect mode (only the `title` tooltip updates) — cosmetic, but it's a view toggle that doesn't say which view you're in.
- **Per-frame, per-creature gradient cost in the browser path:** `springDraw()` (`render.js` L2831) does a `createRadialGradient` + `'multiply'` composite *per creature per frame*, and `drawFireGlow()` does radial gradients per burning cell per frame — on top of the every-frame paint-space overlay set (water shimmer, blades, leaves, falling leaves, motes, ripples). The code's own comments mention software-rasterizer hitches; at phone DPR this per-frame load is significant and none of it is in the cached path.
- **`portraitFor()` runs per creature per frame, not per tick** (`creatureOpt()` inside `render()`): ~2,000 portrait evaluations/sec at 60fps/34 creatures, each doing `driveLevels()` + ~20 trig ops, just to feed the interpolator. Cheap individually, pure waste at that cadence.
- **The page boots at tick 600 = dawn** — the dimmest readable light. First impression of the world is at its darkest.
- **On the phone, ~40% of the frame is empty sky** — the follow camera centers `founder.y − 120` with `VIEW_H=560`, so the top of the frame is vacant gradient while the creature huddles at the bottom third.
- **What my stills could NOT verify (gap, needs a real browser):** the round-3 ground/trees/sky. The legacy stills show chunky per-cell terrain blocks, mud-column trunks the same brown as the soil, soap-bubble leaf-highlight clusters, and hard-edged vivid-blue rectangles where terrain is missing — several of these rhyme with Joshua's "world doesn't look nice," but the browser path rewrote trees, terrain body, and sky, so I cannot say which of these he actually sees. The creature verdict is unaffected (shared art path). Do not treat the stills' terrain as evidence about the live page.
- Inert-but-present: `window.__loam` and `window.__loamProbe` test hooks are live on the page; both are read-only/inert unless activated. No issue, just noting I saw them.

## Bottom line for the builder

The sim is fine; the presentation fights it on three fronts: (1) the frame loop does up to ~120ms of synchronous work per frame and schedules a full-scene repaint every few seconds — no creature art or terrain polish can read through that; (2) the camera shows creatures at 5–9% of screen height, dark-on-dark, never named — the eleven-species roster is experienced as one blob; (3) several controls are dead or misleading in the states players actually use them (paused, on touch, Lab view, new-world). Fix #1 first: until the main thread breathes, everything else is paint on a shaking canvas.
